Human Respiratory System

📘 Standard 7 – Tanzania Syllabus (Science & Health)

1. Meaning of Human Respiratory System

➡️ The human respiratory system is the group of organs and structures that work together to take in oxygen (O₂) from the air and remove carbon dioxide (CO₂) from the body.

It is also called the breathing system. Breathing (external respiration) brings air into the lungs and pushes it out. Inside the lungs, oxygen enters the blood, and carbon dioxide leaves the blood to be breathed out. This process is called gas exchange.

4. Function of Each Part of the Human Respiratory System

👃 Nose nasal cavity
Filters dust and germs with tiny hairs and mucus; warms and moistens the air before it goes to the lungs.
👄 Mouth oral cavity
Alternative way for air to enter and leave the body; does not filter or warm air as well as the nose.
🗣️ Pharynx throat
Passage for air from the nose/mouth to the larynx; also helps in swallowing.
🔊 Larynx voice box
Produces sound (voice) when air passes through the vocal cords; connects pharynx to trachea.
🫁 Trachea windpipe
Carries air from the larynx to the bronchi. It is lined with cilia and mucus to trap dust.
🌿 Bronchi 2 branches
Two main tubes (left and right) that carry air from the trachea into each lung.
🌱 Bronchioles small tubes
Smaller branches inside the lungs that distribute air to all parts of the lungs and lead to alveoli.
🎈 Lungs main organs
Pair of spongy organs where gas exchange happens. They contain bronchi, bronchioles and alveoli.
🔵 Alveoli air sacs
Tiny air sacs surrounded by blood capillaries. Oxygen passes into the blood, and carbon dioxide leaves the blood to be breathed out.
🛡️ Diaphragm muscle
Dome-shaped muscle below the lungs. It contracts (flattens) to pull air in (inhale) and relaxes to push air out (exhale).
🦴 Ribs rib cage
Bony cage that protects the lungs and heart. They move during breathing to help air enter and leave.
💪 Intercostal muscles
Muscles between the ribs. They lift the rib cage up and out during inhalation, and lower it during exhalation.
📌 Tanzania Std 7 reminder: The respiratory system works together with the circulatory system to deliver oxygen to all body cells and remove carbon dioxide.
➡️ Breathing in (inhalation): diaphragm contracts & moves down, ribs move up & out → air rushes into lungs.
➡️ Breathing out (exhalation): diaphragm relaxes & moves up, ribs move down & in → air is pushed out.
🌿 Gas exchange takes place in the alveoli.
